What to fix, in order

  1. 1CriticalRule allows any source to reach any destination on any port ×3Replace it with rules for the sources, destinations and services people actually need. If it was added as a temporary fix, remove it.
  2. 2CriticalFirmware has a vulnerability that attackers are known to use ×2Upgrade to a fixed release as soon as you can. Until then, turn off or restrict the affected feature and check the firewall for signs of compromise as the advisory describes.
  3. 3CriticalFirewall management is reachable from the internetRemove https, http, ssh, telnet and snmp from allowaccess on internet-facing interfaces. If you have to manage over the internet, limit sources with a local-in policy or use a VPN.
  4. 4CriticalRule never takes effect because an earlier rule does the oppositeMove this rule above the broader one, or narrow the broader rule so it no longer covers this traffic.
  5. 5HighTraffic is allowed without any security inspection ×3Attach security profiles to the rule, at least IPS and antivirus, and make sure utm-status is enabled.
  6. 6HighInternet traffic can reach internal services too broadlyPublish only what must be public, through VIPs. Restrict sources where you can and put IPS on the rule. Admin protocols such as RDP and SSH belong behind a VPN.
  7. 7HighAdmin accounts without two-factor authenticationEnable two-factor authentication (FortiToken, email or SMS) for every admin, or enforce MFA on the RADIUS or LDAP server that authenticates them.
  8. 8HighAdmin accounts that can log in from anywhereSet trusthost entries to your management networks. Trusted hosts only help if every admin account has them, and they don't stop attacks that happen before login, so also restrict the admin interfaces with a local-in policy.
  9. 9HighUnencrypted admin access (HTTP or Telnet)Remove telnet and http from allowaccess on every interface and use SSH and HTTPS only.
  10. 10HighStrong cryptography is turned offTurn it back on: config system global / set strong-crypto enable.
  11. 11HighService reachable from the internet without any inspection ×2Add IPS to the rule (and antivirus where files are exchanged), use deep inspection if the service uses TLS and you can load its certificate, and limit sources where you can.
  12. 12HighSSL-VPN portal open to the internetKeep the firmware on a fixed release, require MFA, restrict source addresses or countries, and consider moving users to IPsec VPN or ZTNA.
  13. 13HighScanners can't see inside encrypted trafficUse a deep-inspection SSL/SSH profile on rules where you rely on these scanners, with exemptions for sensitive categories such as banking and health.
  14. 14HighTraffic between interfaces in a zone isn't filteredSet intrazone deny and write rules for the traffic that should flow, or split unrelated networks into separate zones.
  15. 15MediumTraffic logging is turned off on an allow ruleSet logtraffic to all, or at least utm.
  16. 16MediumWeak password rules for adminsEnable the admin password policy with a minimum length of 12 and at least two character types (config system password-policy).
  17. 17MediumSeveral accounts with full super_admin rightsKeep super_admin to one or two accounts and give everyone else a profile limited to their job.
  18. 18MediumLogs aren't sent off the firewallSend logs to FortiAnalyzer, FortiAnalyzer Cloud or a syslog/SIEM server.
  19. 19MediumDeep inspection uses the built-in Fortinet CAIssue a subordinate CA from your own PKI for SSL inspection and push it to clients.
  20. 20MediumIPS sensor only watches, never blocksSet the action to block for high and critical signatures once you've checked for false positives.
  21. 21LowDefault 'admin' account still existsCreate personal admin accounts, then rename or delete the default 'admin' account.
  22. 22LowAdmin sessions stay open too longSet admintimeout to a short value, such as 5 to 10 minutes.
  23. 23LowShort lockout after failed admin loginsSet admin-lockout-threshold to 3 and admin-lockout-duration to 900 seconds or more.
  24. 24LowSSL-VPN uses the factory certificateInstall a certificate from a trusted CA for the portal's hostname and set it as servercert.
  25. 25LowDuplicate ruleDelete the later copy.
